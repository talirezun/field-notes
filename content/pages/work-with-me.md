## What I do

Four things, in roughly the order they take up my time. If one of them fits,
the fastest route is a message on [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/talirezun/).

## Teaching

I lecture on AI for executive and MBA cohorts at COTRUGLI Business School, and I
deliver professional certification programmes. The material is the same material
that ends up here: how these systems work, what they cost, what fails, and what
an organisation actually has to change to get value out of them.

Some of this is delivered in Slovenian. Ask if that matters for your programme.

## Advisory

I work with organisations on AI adoption, mostly in Europe, mostly at the point
where a pilot has to become a system. That work is covered on
[talirezun.com](https://talirezun.com), which is the better place to look if you
want the formal version.

The honest description of what I am useful for: deciding what to build, deciding
what not to build, and telling you which of the two problems you actually have.

## Building

I build and run software. [The Curator](/the-curator) is open source and MIT
licensed. [Lumina](https://luminawidget.xyz) is a commercial product. Chapter
nine covers both in the terms I am willing to state publicly.

I am not available for contract development.

## Speaking

Conferences, internal leadership sessions, panels. I speak about what I have
built and what it cost, which tends to be a different talk from the one people
expect.
